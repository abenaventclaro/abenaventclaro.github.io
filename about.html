About

Between equations and experiments

I am a soft matter physicist working mainly on fluid dynamics, microfluidics and interfacial phenomena. My research moves continuously between theory and experiment: I like problems where a simple physical model can be tested directly against a carefully controlled system.

During my PhD at the University of Barcelona I worked on fluid-front dynamics, microfluidic pumping, imbibition, confined multiphase flows and rheological measurement. I have also worked in industrial R&D and university teaching, experiences that shaped the way I approach research problems from both fundamental and practical perspectives.

My current research focuses on particle detachment by moving interfaces and flowing liquid films. I am particularly interested in identifying the simplest physical ingredients that determine whether a particle remains attached, rolls, slides or detaches.

What I enjoy most in physics is the point where an equation stops being only a description and becomes something that can be confronted with an experiment.
